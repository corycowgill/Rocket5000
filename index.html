    DOC 5000-B MISSION BRIEFING

    BUILDING

    Click a part on the left, then click on the rocket grid to place it. Stack parts vertically. You need at least one engine, one fuel tank, and one body.

    FLYING

    Hold Space or THRUST to burn fuel. Tap ←/→ to steer. Don't oversteer or you'll spin out.

    STAGING

    Press S or the STAGE button to drop the bottom-most fuel tank (and anything below it) for a kick. Drop empty tanks to shed dead weight. Drop too early and you waste fuel.

    DODGE FOR COMBO

    Tilt to dodge birds, debris, and lightning. Each near-miss adds to your PILOT combo — a scrap multiplier that resets when you take a hit.

    WEATHER

    Each launch rolls a weather condition. METEOR SHOWER, AURORA, and THUNDERSTORM pay more scrap but raise the risk. TAILWIND is safer but pays less.

    WINNING

    Reach 1,000,000 ft to touch the moon. Crashes earn scrap from altitude, milestones, and pilot combo. Higher altitudes unlock better parts.

    JANK

    Some parts are unreliable. Higher Jank means weirder flights.
