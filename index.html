        ACHIEVEMENTS

        DOC 5000-B MISSION BRIEFING

        BUILDING

        Click a part on the left, then click on the rocket grid to place it. Stack parts vertically. You need at least one engine, one fuel tank, and one body.

        BUILD SLOTS

        The hangar has three save slots: A / B / C. Switch between them with the tabs above the rocket name. Each slot is autosaved as you edit so you can keep your moonshot build untouched while experimenting in another slot.

        FLYING

        Hold Space or THRUST to burn fuel. Tap ←/→ to steer. Don't oversteer or you'll spin out.

        STAGING

        Build stages by stacking engine → fuel → engine → fuel in the hangar: an engine placed on top of a fuel tank starts the next stage up. Press S or the STAGE button to drop the spent bottom stage for a kick — the engine above keeps firing. Shed dead weight once a stage burns dry, but drop too early and you waste fuel. Watch the STAGES ladder on the left — the active stage drains green→empty; drop it right as it empties for a CLEAN SEPARATION scrap bonus.

        DODGE FOR COMBO

        Tilt to dodge birds, debris, and lightning. Each near-miss adds to your PILOT combo — a scrap multiplier that resets when you take a hit.

        WEATHER

        Each launch rolls a weather condition. METEOR SHOWER, AURORA, and THUNDERSTORM pay more scrap but raise the risk. TAILWIND is safer but pays less.

        PICKUPS

        Floating items spawn ahead of the rocket: scrap cubes (+50), fuel cans (+20% fuel), repair kits (+30 hull), data crystals (+3 data), and rare gold stars (+200). They drift toward you within range — steer to grab them.

        WINNING

        Reach 1,000,000 ft to touch the moon. Crashes earn scrap from altitude, milestones, and pilot combo. Higher altitudes unlock better parts.

        JANK

        Some parts are unreliable. Higher Jank means weirder flights.
